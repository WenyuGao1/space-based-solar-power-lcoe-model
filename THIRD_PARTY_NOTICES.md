# Third-Party Data and Sources

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` applies to the original software and project-authored documentation in this repository. It does not replace the licences or terms that apply to third-party source material.

The raw UK government workbooks in `data/raw/` are redistributed for reproducibility and retain their original source status. UK government material is generally available under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), except where the source states otherwise.

The source, organisation, publication date, URL, analytical role and notes for each external source are recorded in `data/source_registry.csv`. Users redistributing or reusing third-party material should verify the terms on the linked source page.

References to external papers and reports do not imply that their authors or organisations endorse this assessment.
