Methodological interpretation

What the interactive result means

The controls above are the interactive surface. The sections below state the accounting boundary and the evidence limits needed to interpret the number rigorously.

Correct mass basis

Orbital mass equals delivered kW multiplied by kg/kW-delivered. It is not divided by efficiency again. At 0.67 kW/kg, a 2 GW system is about 2,985 tonnes.

Replacement boundaries

Space replacement includes eligible hardware plus launch, transfer and assembly. Ground replacement applies only to rectenna and grid assets. Contingency is not charged again each year.

Dependence warning

Efficiency, specific mass, hardware cost, launch cadence, replacement logistics and financing risk may be correlated. Equal-fraction presets are mathematical comparisons, not roadmaps.
