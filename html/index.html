Current conditional LCOE—

Explicit discounted cash flow, expressed per MWh of AC delivered to the grid boundary.

Delivered-power mass basis—

Delivered kW multiplied by kg/kW-delivered. Efficiency is not divided a second time.

Stage-resolved chain—

Five independently adjustable conversion stages, calculated backwards from delivered AC.

Deployment diagnostic—

Equivalent launch count reflects payload and utilisation; it is not a manifest or schedule.

Accounting boundary

AC at the grid connection point; 2024 real GBP; valuation at start of construction. Construction duration and degradation are explicit.

Physical limitations

Beam geometry, spectrum, land, weather, safety, thermal design, debris, logistics and detailed replacement missions are not physically resolved.

Evidence discipline

All 29 defaults are study-authored exploratory assumptions. External studies provide context, range inspiration and consistency checks—not direct substitution.
